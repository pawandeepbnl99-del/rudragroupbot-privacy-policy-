Effective Date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RudraGroupBot collects, uses, stores and protects information when users interact with the bot on Telegram.

Depending on the features used, RudraGroupBot may process:

  • Telegram user IDs and group IDs.
  • Username, display name and basic profile information made available through Telegram.
  • Messages, commands and relevant group conversation context sent to or processed by the bot.
  • Game scores, preferences, tagging interactions and feature settings.
  • Reminder details and information users voluntarily provide.

Information may be used to:

  • Provide bot commands and functionality.
  • Respond to messages and AI interactions.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the functionality of the bot.

6. User Rights and Requests

Users may request information about their stored data or request deletion of data associated with their use of the bot, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal requirements.
